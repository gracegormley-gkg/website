About the Project

The EIS Archives is a digital humanities project at Northwestern University's Knight Lab that makes 115 Environmental Impact Statements searchable and explorable for the first time. The collection spans 10 major themes and 17 subthemes, organized around climate change narratives drawn from federal environmental documents. Visitors can explore an interactive theme network, a geographic map of all documents, and curated thematic exhibitions.
