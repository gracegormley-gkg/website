SUSO Camp

Stand Up, Speak Out: Journalism for Young Activists

A year-long project teaching journalism to middle schoolers across three grade levels. Students wrote and published articles, read them aloud, and saw their work compiled into 200+ printed magazines. The camp continued independently in 2024, carried forward by DreamCatchers without my direct involvement.
