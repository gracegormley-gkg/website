SUSO Camp

Stand Up, Speak Out: Journalism for Young Activists

Overview

Stand Up, Speak Out: Journalism for Young Activists is a project I undertook for my Girl Scout Gold Award — the highest honor in Girl Scouting, awarded for sustainable community impact. I wanted to address problems in journalism by combining my passions for teaching, public speaking, service, and journalism.

The project took almost an entire year and more than 100 hours of work.

The Four Pillars

Writing

Over 70 students wrote articles with research and peer quotes about topics they care about, including teen homelessness and climate change.

Public Speaking

Students rehearsed and read their articles aloud to classrooms, building confidence in public speaking.

Results

Student stories were compiled into three printed magazines (one per grade). Nearly $1,000 was fundraised to print over 200 total magazines for students to take home.

Longevity

I created a website with all resources needed to replicate the camp, worked with local organizations to ensure continuation, and contacted dozens of journalism teachers to spread SUSO nationwide. The camp continued in 2024 with DreamCatchers without my direct involvement.

Impact

Across three grade levels, SUSO Camp reached approximately 70 students. Student articles were compiled into three printed magazines — over 200 copies total, funded by nearly $1,000 in fundraising. The camp's reach extended beyond me: in 2024, DreamCatchers continued SUSO Camp independently, demonstrating the program's lasting design.
